# Enterprise AI Quality Role Competency Map

This map connects the target role to learning, practice, Evalorium artifacts, and evidence. It guides development; it does not guarantee hiring or replace employment history.

## Responsibilities

| Role responsibility | Required learning | Practical evidence | Evalorium artifact | Verification | Current state |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise AI quality standards | Quality models, risk taxonomy, baselines, ownership | Define standards for a multi-component AI product | Standards and policy modules | Scenario review, consistency checks, gate decisions | `planned` |
| Mandatory release quality gates | Decision theory, thresholds, uncertainty, exceptions | Build PR, CI/CD, canary, rollback, and waiver gates | Quality Gates | Regression demonstrations and audit trail | `planned` |
| Production risk monitoring | Drift, hallucination, bias, incident response, sampling | Detect and reproduce a production degradation | Observability and incident workflow | Detection quality, replay, new regression case | `planned` |
| Enterprise evaluation framework | Functional accuracy, edge cases, consistency, safety | Design a multi-dimensional framework from requirements | Eval Core and Measurement | Reproducibility, coverage, statistics, limitations | `planned` |
| Automated CI/CD evaluation | Versioning, caching, budgets, comparison, failure policy | Block a harmful candidate and permit a safe change | Runner and CI integration | Automated tests and reviewed gate outcome | `planned` |
| Frontier evaluation methods | Judge reliability, automated red team, agent evaluation | Run calibration, adversarial, and trajectory studies | Judge, Red Team, Agent Harness | Human comparison, attack evidence, environment state | `planned` |
| Forward-looking risk analysis | Multimodal failure, alignment loss, systemic risk | Convert an emerging risk into tests and policy | Risk register and regression suites | Expert review, incident simulation, coverage update | `planned` |

## Requirements

| Role requirement | Repository-supported evidence | Evidence the repository cannot manufacture | Current state |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6+ years software engineering; 2+ years real AI evaluation delivery | Architecture, code quality, releases, issue history, and sustained framework evolution | Employment tenure and duration of real enterprise ownership | External evidence required |
| Independent multi-dimensional evaluation design | Standards, datasets, scorers, statistical plan, safety coverage, and capstone defense | Performance in an employer-specific domain without access | `planned` |
| LLM-as-Judge, human annotation, automated metrics, and bias control | Calibration studies, annotation protocol, agreement analysis, metric validation, and bias tests | Proprietary labeling operations not represented publicly | `planned` |
| CI/CD quality gate design | Reproducible pipeline, baselines, uncertainty-aware policy, waivers, and rollback | Actual enterprise release authority | `planned` |
| Quality culture and business-validated organizational impact | Governance templates, contributor process, adoption playbook, and public case studies | Real cross-team influence, accountable adoption, and business outcomes | External evidence required |

## Evidence rule

Each `planned` row advances only when its linked artifact, tests, analysis, and limitations exist. Learning notes alone do not prove professional delivery. Years of experience, organizational leadership, and business validation must remain accurately represented in a résumé and interview.
