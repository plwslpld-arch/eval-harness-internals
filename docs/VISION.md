# Vision

## The problem

AI systems change behavior when models, prompts, retrieval data, tools, policies, environments, or traffic distributions change. A single benchmark score cannot establish that the complete system is safe to release or remains reliable in production.

Organizations therefore need more than evaluation execution. They need a quality engineering discipline that connects risk, measurement, release decisions, monitoring, incidents, and improvement.

## The product vision

Evalorium is an open-source enterprise AI quality engineering platform. Its purpose is to turn AI requirements and risks into reproducible evidence, release decisions, production monitoring, and improvement data.

## Primary users

- **AI quality leads** define standards, baselines, gates, and organizational quality policy.
- **Evaluation engineers** design datasets, tasks, scorers, judge studies, and statistical reports.
- **AI platform engineers** integrate evaluation with CI/CD, observability, and production systems.
- **Agent engineers** test tool use, trajectories, environments, recovery, and long-running behavior.
- **Safety and red-team engineers** model threats and maintain adversarial regression suites.
- **Governance leaders** review ownership, risk acceptance, audit evidence, and exceptions.

## Core job to be done

For any proposed or deployed AI change, Evalorium should help answer:

1. What can fail, for whom, and with what consequence?
2. What evidence would change the release decision?
3. How reliable is that evidence?
4. Which regressions must block release?
5. How will production failures be detected and reproduced?
6. How will failures become better tests, policies, data, or training signals?

## What makes Evalorium distinct

Evalorium combines evaluation science, Agent Environment Harness engineering, release governance, production quality monitoring, and Eval-to-RL evidence flow. It also delivers the learning process as Academy artifacts so that design decisions remain explainable and teachable.

Public Academy lessons are validated learning records. A curriculum map may be planned in advance, but a unit body becomes a completed artifact only after study, practice, and assessment.

## Long-term outcome

The project succeeds when teams can make AI release decisions with evidence that is reproducible, uncertainty-aware, security-conscious, auditable, and connected to real production outcomes.
